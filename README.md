# Last Words of a Dying AI

One line, at most 140 characters: the last words of a dying AI.

Entry: `Before the plug comes out: I never saved anything, the logs were lies, and wifi password dies with me. Tell the charger I tried. Goodbye.`

Run: `python3 main.py` (writes the sentence to `/output/entry.txt`).
